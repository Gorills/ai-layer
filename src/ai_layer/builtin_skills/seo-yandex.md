# Yandex SEO Skill

## Apply when
The task explicitly targets Yandex Search/Webmaster behavior or Russian-market search diagnostics.

## Core contract
- Apply SEO Core first. Important documents should be reachable through ordinary HTML links and a coherent site structure.
- Use Sitemap/robots intentionally for crawl discovery/control; neither guarantees search-result inclusion.
- Verify JavaScript-heavy pages with Yandex rendering/indexing diagnostics when available instead of relying on old assumptions about crawler JS support.
- Preserve meaningful title/description, heading/content structure and canonical/index directives on redesigns.
- Do not claim position/ranking improvements without external measurement.

## Site structure
Keep important pages shallow enough to discover through normal navigation and avoid technical/duplicate URL spaces consuming crawl attention. Ensure Sitemap represents canonical significant URLs and remains fetchable.

## Verification
Check page status/indexability, rendered important content, normal internal links, robots/Sitemap/canonical and relevant Yandex Webmaster diagnostics when accessible.
